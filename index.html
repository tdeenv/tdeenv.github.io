Professional Experience

Social Impact & Property

I currently work as a Social Sustainability Analyst at Stockland, where I focus on social impact measurement and community development within the property sector.

Public Policy & Evaluation

In 2019, during my first year at university, I joined ARTD Consultants—a boutique public policy consulting firm—as an intern. I later progressed to a research assistant and then to an analyst role. At ARTD, I worked on evaluations of public policies and programs across a range of sectors, including:

  • Health
  • Transport, infrastructure, and regional development
  • Environment and natural resources
  • Disability and ageing
  • Education and youth development

Working on quantitative analyses in these projects helped me discover a strong interest in working with data, which led me to shift my university major from international business to econometrics.

Market Research

I also worked as an Insights Analyst on a part-time contract with GfK (now part of NielsenIQ) in the consumer intelligence and consulting team. My work involved analysing survey data, developing client reports, assisting with questionnaire design, and maintaining brand health trackers.
