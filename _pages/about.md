About me
=============
I currently work as a social sustainability analyst at Stockland, where I focus on social impact measurement and social value creation.

I graduated from the University of Sydney with a Bachelor of Economics in Economics and Econometrics. I am passionate about driving social impact using quantitative insights.

In my spare time I like listening to music (ambient, techno, synthwave, metal), reading (Brandon Sanderson, sci-fi, economics), and cycling. I also really enjoy making things, either physically with wood or more recently digitally with code.
